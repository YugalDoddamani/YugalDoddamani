A bit about me

Video editing, design, web work, and dental technology.

I started with video editing, then moved into graphic design, web development, and Google Business Profile management for local clients. Each of those taught me something different about visual communication and how digital systems actually work.

Alongside that, I trained and qualified as a dental technologist, specializing in prosthetics and orthodontic appliance fabrication. It's some of the most precise work there is — a crown margin off by 30 micrometers simply fails. That standard is what I bring to everything else I build.

Today I split my time between the technical and digital side of Carnix Smile Studio, the dental lab I co-run, and design, video, and web projects for outside clients. The work looks different case to case, but the approach doesn't change: understand the problem first, then execute carefully.
