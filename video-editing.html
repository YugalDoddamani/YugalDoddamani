Video Post-Production & Audio Sync

Technical timeline assembly, precise audio matching, and visual motion editing built for clean, high-retention media.

Back to Homepage

Editing Specializations

Technical workflows and creative processes for video post-production.

Graphic & Kinetic Typography

Custom text animations, animated lower-thirds, and synchronized dynamic subtitles designed for high legibility and viewer engagement across vertical and landscape formats.

Precision Speed Ramping & Pacing

Smooth speed ramps, time-remapping, and frame-rate adjustments to create seamless transitions and rhythm matching the music beat or action timing.

Audio-Visual Sync & Voiceover Assembly

Multi-track audio alignment, precision lip-syncing, and video trimming tailored around recorded voiceovers, podcasts, and narration tracks.

Motion Graphics Integration

Clean 2D graphics insertion, logo reveals, subtle callouts, and keyframe animations using After Effects to elevate standard cuts without cluttering the screen.

Podcast & Multi-Cam Editing

Timeline structuring for multi-speaker formats, cutting between camera angles based on active audio queues, and applying noise reduction to vocal tracks.

Casual & Commercial Edits

Adaptable video assembly—from clean corporate presentations and vlog-style content to short-form reel/TikTok cuts—focused on pacing, color balance, and clean cuts.

// Workflow

Post-Production Pipeline

01 Media Organization

Sorting raw A-roll/B-roll, syncing scratch audio to high-quality vocal tracks, and setting up sequence settings.

02 Rough Cut & Assembly

Trimming fluff, enforcing narrative pacing, and locking down the core timeline.

03 Motion, Text & Audio Polish

Adding kinetic text, applying speed ramps, integrating motion graphics, and balancing multi-channel audio.

04 Final Render & Export

Rendering high-bitrate outputs optimized for web, YouTube, or mobile platform specifications.

▸ Adobe After Effects (Kinetic Typography, Keyframing & Motion Graphics)
